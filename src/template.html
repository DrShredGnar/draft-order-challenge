Draft Order Challenge

On the clock 12 picks Decided on the field, not by a random number generator.

Twelve questions, four tiers, thirty seconds each. Every question is drawn at random from a sealed pool at kickoff — the commissioner plays as blind as everyone else. The final scoreboard becomes the draft board — twelve questions decide the draft order, and the winner takes first pick.

One of every three comes from this league's own history — the 2024 and 2025 drafts, both title games, every weekly score. The draw happens on the host device the moment the game starts, so the commissioner is playing the same unseen set as the other eleven.
